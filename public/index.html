How to play

  1. The green curve is the mystery function. Its equation is shown below the graph with the numbers blanked out.
  2. Fill in the boxes and take a shot. Your guess draws in orange on the same graph so you can compare.
  3. The hint line lists every value each box can take. A value that isn't on the list is rejected without costing you a stroke.
  4. Every shot costs 1 stroke. Match the curve exactly to sink the hole and move on. Each hole gets harder.
  5. Assists cost strokes too: Axis marks +1 and Window +1, charged once per hole.
  6. Stuck? Pick up to see the answer and take par + 5.
  7. 18 holes, par 72. Lowest score wins, just like golf.

Tips: the hint line lists every possible value. Fractions like 1/2 work. On a phone, use ± Sign to make a number negative.
